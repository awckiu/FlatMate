                 Notice period
                
                
                Most tenants have to give at least two months' notice in writing, and it has to end at the end of a rental period: the day before your rent day (the day of the month your tenancy started), or on the rent day itself. So once the two months are up you still finish out that rental month. Check your agreement for the exact period and any fixed term or break clause.

Under the Tenant Fees Act 2019 a landlord can't charge you a set professional-cleaning fee; they can only deduct the actual cost of putting right anything beyond fair wear and tear.
