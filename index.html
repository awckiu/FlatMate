Your agreement, decoded

Upload a PDF, add the lead tenant’s name, any co-tenants and the property address. When Gemini is configured, the review can summarize key terms and easy-to-miss need-to-knows—like guest rules, permitted activities and fees or charges—then flag points to clarify and possible legal contradictions supported by available sources. If issues are flagged, it drafts a landlord email for you to copy and paste; Gmail and Outlook sending aren’t connected.

Planned · law sources needed

Grounded UK law checks

A maintained UK housing-law library—including relevant Housing Act and renters’ rights material—will power retrieval-augmented answers. Guardrails should keep each check specific to the agreement and relevant retrieved sections, cite sources, and label a concern as something to clarify when it can’t be verified, rather than guessing.

Planned · shared profile

One home, the right setup

Choose solo, family or friends—including a tenancy swap—and whether the home is an HMO or not; set what’s included so need-to-knows fit your situation. Say whether you’re the lead tenant or a co-tenant. A shared tenancy profile can keep different responsibilities, like who owns the energy account, clear. A code for your specific tenancy could unlock the mobile experience when it starts.

Planned · bills & area info

Make bills less of a surprise

Switch off what’s already included, then plan around UK regional estimates, an EPC rating checker, average energy prices and price changes—with alerts if bills rise. Keep an eye on council tax, Wi-Fi, parking permits and bike costs. Future Tiger Data area insights could highlight common property issues around an address where reliable data is available.

Planned · dates & reminders

Stay one step ahead

A tenancy calendar could remind you about bill payments and meter readings. When it’s time to leave, it can calculate the notice window (for example, two months where your agreement requires it) and check the agreement and applicable rules, then lay out deposit, cleaning and move-out steps.

Planned · mobile app

Keep a record of your place

Use your phone camera to save move-in photos alongside your inventory, so you have a useful record to refer back to for maintenance requests, landlord conversations and deposit questions.

Planned · tenancy buddy

A buddy for the in-between bits

After signing, tell a tenancy chatbot what’s happening and get help understanding next steps. Later, you could upload landlord emails for interpretation or opt in to email access. With your say-so, it could prepare a landlord message for your chosen channel; sending requires your review and an email or text integration.

Exploration · adaptable help

Advice that keeps up with you

Longer term, adaptable AI helpers could respond to changing legislation and your changing priorities, while keeping suggestions specific to your tenancy and settings. Incident.io is an exploration here—not a live FlatMate feature or a promised integration.

Gemini-powered analysis RAG over sourced legislation Tiger Data for vector search and future area insights

A note on what’s live: the current app reviews the agreement and can draft an email, but it doesn’t yet save need-to-knows into a persistent tenancy profile or provide the planned bills, calendar, camera, shared-app or chat features. Legal contradictions can only be checked against law sections that have actually been added to the reference library; that source library is not bundled yet.
