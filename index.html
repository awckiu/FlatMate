Stage 1 · Before you sign

Make sense of the fine print

Review your agreement in plain English, check important details, and prepare questions to clarify before you commit. Legal checks depend on relevant law sources being available.

Stage 2 · Moving in

Start your tenancy with a clear record

Confirm your signing and start dates, share the tenancy code with named co-tenants, and save dated room photos as a move-in condition record. Photos are stored as evidence and are not analysed by AI.

Stage 3 · Living together

Keep shared tenancy details together

Track rent and bill due dates, set optional email reminders, and ask the tenancy buddy about issues. The buddy can prepare landlord-message drafts, but it does not send them.

Stage 4 · Moving on

Plan a smoother move-out

Use your agreement to check notice requirements and dates, agree a clear handover, document the property’s condition, and keep records about cleaning and your deposit.

A practical move-out checklist

Move-out planning tools are not connected yet. For now, use this checklist as a starting point and verify each step against your agreement and local advice.

  • Check the agreement and get advice if you’re unsure about your notice period or how to serve notice.
  • Agree a move-out date and key handover with your landlord or agent in writing.
  • Take dated photos, record meter readings, and keep copies of relevant messages.
  • Ask for the deposit-protection scheme’s return process and dispute deadlines.

Make bills less of a surprise

Available · optional email reminders

Stay one step ahead

Track one-off or monthly rent and bill due dates in your tenancy calendar. Optional email reminders can be sent two days before a due date when the site administrator has configured email delivery. Notice-window calculations and a guided move-out plan are not connected yet.

A buddy for the in-between bits

After signing, ask the tenancy buddy about your agreement and relevant retrieved law. It can prepare an email or text-message draft for you to review and send. Automatic landlord contact, email access and message sending are not connected.
